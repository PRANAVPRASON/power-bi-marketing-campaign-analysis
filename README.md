# Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing marketing campaign performance using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into campaign ROI, revenue, marketing expenditure, regional performance, and campaign types.

The objective is to evaluate marketing effectiveness and identify the campaigns generating the highest returns.

## Dashboard Preview
![Marketing Campaign Dashboard](Screenshot%202026-11-18%20125611.png)

The dashboard provides a visual overview of marketing performance through interactive charts and KPI cards.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Average ROI | 0.68 |
| Total Revenue | 42.54M |
| Total Spend | 25.69M |
| Best Performing Campaign | Influencer Marketing |

## Key Analysis

**1. Regional Performance**
- Compared marketing spending and revenue across different regions.
- Analyzed performance across Africa, Asia, North America, Europe, Oceania, and South America.

**2. Campaign Performance**
- Compared total ROI across different marketing campaigns.
- Identified Influencer Marketing as the highest-performing campaign based on total ROI.

**3. Campaign Spending & ROI**
- Analyzed campaign-wise marketing expenditure.
- Compared total spend with average ROI.

**4. Campaign Type Analysis**
- Compared Digital and Traditional marketing campaigns.
- Digital campaigns: 5 (62.5%)
- Traditional campaigns: 3 (37.5%)

## Key Insights
- Influencer Marketing achieved the highest total ROI among the analyzed campaigns.
- Digital campaigns represent 62.5% of the campaign types.
- Total marketing revenue was 42.54M against a total spend of 25.69M.
- Regional analysis provides a comparison of marketing spending and revenue across six regions.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Power BI can be used to analyze marketing campaign performance, evaluate ROI, compare regional results, and generate meaningful business insights through interactive data visualizations.
